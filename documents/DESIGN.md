# SharpShooter — Design Document

## Concept

This game is a third-person shooter that revolves around the player attempting to defend themselves from flying birds. The game allows the player to shoot at the bird and to jump on them in order to damage the birds. The birds also have different attacks and can fly.

## How to Play

The player can control the character by using the WASD keys and clicking at the bird in order to shoot it. They can move the camera by moving their cursor.
