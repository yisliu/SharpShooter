# SharpShooter

This is a 3D low poly third person shooter game. The player plays as a pink haired character who needs to defeat evil birds. The player can shoot at the birds, but also stomp on them. The bird enemies have a variety of different attack patterns and can fly.

## Gameplay

- Move through a forest and defeat birds
- Defeat the birds by shooting at them or jumping on them
- Defeating the birds and taking in the cooked chicken allows you to regain health.

## Controls

You move with WASD keys and shoot by clicking. You can move the camera by moving your cursor.

## How to Play

Open up the game and move the WASD keys. You need to defeat all of the evil birds without dying to win.
